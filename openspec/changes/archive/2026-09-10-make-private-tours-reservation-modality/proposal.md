## Why

Private tours are currently presented as a separate commercial section while listing the same published routes as the normal tour catalog. This creates duplicate discovery paths and makes "private" look like a different product instead of a booking modality for the same routes.

## What Changes

- Treat private group booking as an option available from each eligible published tour card and tour detail page.
- Remove `Tours privados` / `Private tours` as a primary navigation section or parallel catalog entry point.
- Preserve external WhatsApp and email reservation flows, with private-group wording when the visitor books a published route privately.
- Allow SEO landing pages for private-guide/private-tour search intent only as contextual support pages, not as a separate product catalog or main navigation section.
- Update internal links so visitors are guided toward the main tours catalog, relevant tour detail pages, tailor-made inquiry, contact, or SEO context pages without duplicating route content.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `static-tourism-site`: Private tour behavior changes from a separate visible section/catalog to a reservation modality attached to published tours, while preserving useful SEO landing coverage.

## Impact

- Affected public pages: localized home pages, tour cards, tour detail pages, navigation, sitemap/internal links, and private-tour SEO landing/discovery pages.
- Affected content/copy: Spanish and English labels and reservation wording for private group booking.
- No new dependencies, no internal booking/payment system, and no change to the data-driven tour content model unless implementation needs a minimal metadata flag for private eligibility.
