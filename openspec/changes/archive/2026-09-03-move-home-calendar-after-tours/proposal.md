## Why

The reservation calendar is currently intended for discovery, but it appears before visitors have seen the tour catalog. Moving it below the tours makes the home page flow clearer: discover routes first, then choose an available date.

## What Changes

- Move the localized home reservation calendar so it appears after the home tour catalog.
- Preserve the existing calendar content, schedule rules, email reservation links, and route-specific tour detail calendars.
- Keep the rest of the home page order unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `static-tourism-site`: Clarifies the required placement of the localized home reservation calendar relative to the tour catalog.

## Impact

- Affects only the localized home page section order.
- Does not change data models, routes, reservation email behavior, tour detail calendars, dependencies, or deployment configuration.
