## Why

Visitors who need help planning their Granada visit or want a route adapted to specific interests do not currently have a dedicated path beyond choosing an existing tour. Adding a tailor-made offer creates a clear conversion path for consultation and personalized Granada routes while preserving the site's lightweight external-reservation model.

## What Changes

- Add a localized home-page section after the tour catalog and before traveler reviews promoting tailor-made trips and routes.
- Add localized standalone pages for the offer at `/es/viajes-a-medida/` and `/en/tailor-made-trips/`.
- Present two service paths: travel consultation for Granada planning and personalized private routes in Granada.
- Include clear external WhatsApp calls to action with prefilled inquiry text instead of an internal form, checkout, or payment flow.
- Add appropriate localized navigation/discovery links and SEO metadata for the new pages.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `static-tourism-site`: Extend the public tourism website with a localized tailor-made trips/routes discovery and inquiry experience.

## Impact

- Affected pages: localized home page and new localized tailor-made offer pages.
- Affected shared content: localized labels/copy, navigation/discovery links, SEO metadata, and WhatsApp CTA text.
- No new backend, database, payment, account, or internal booking dependencies.
