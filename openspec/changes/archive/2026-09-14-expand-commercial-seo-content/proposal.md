## Why

Elyara already has a solid technical SEO foundation, but many commercial tour, landing, and editorial pages are still too brief to compete for priority Granada search intents. The next opportunity is to deepen useful visitor-facing content, strengthen topical clusters, improve image performance, and prepare Google Business Profile integration without making unsupported ranking, review, credential, or business-location claims.

## What Changes

- Expand priority Spanish and English tour detail pages with richer visitor guidance: route focus, practical fit, pace, context, meeting/ending details when known, private-group fit, and FAQs.
- Expand priority commercial SEO landing pages so they answer distinct search intents rather than repeating thin tour descriptions.
- Strengthen editorial topic clusters with substantial Spanish and English articles that support commercial discovery and link to relevant tours, landings, contact, and tailor-made inquiry paths.
- Improve image performance for large public assets by using appropriately sized modern formats for SEO-critical pages while preserving accessible alt text and visual meaning.
- Prepare verified Google Business Profile integration so an approved URL can be surfaced visibly and included in structured data later, without publishing an unverified profile claim.
- Preserve current constraints: no fake reviews, no invented ratings, no unsupported credentials, no internal booking/payment flow, and no guarantee of first-page rankings.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `static-tourism-site`: Expand requirements for substantial tour-detail and commercial landing content, plus SEO-safe image performance behavior for public pages.
- `editorial-blog`: Expand requirements for clustered, substantial editorial content that supports route and commercial discovery without thin or duplicate pages.
- `local-seo-presence`: Expand requirements for Google Business Profile integration readiness, image/performance trust signals, and ranking-claim safeguards.

## Impact

- Affected content: tour Markdown entries, SEO landing Markdown entries, editorial blog entries, FAQs, internal links, and localized metadata in Spanish and English.
- Affected UI: public tour detail pages, SEO landing pages, blog pages, and possibly shared image rendering patterns.
- Affected structured data: may include approved visible Google Business Profile URL later, but only after the URL is approved and visibly linked on public pages.
- Affected assets: large JPEG/PNG public images may gain optimized WebP/AVIF replacements or page-specific resized variants.
- No dependency on an internal booking system, payment system, fake review schema, aggregate rating schema, or guaranteed ranking claim.
