## Why

The site already has a strong technical SEO foundation, but the next ranking opportunity is trust: Search Console ownership, legally credible public pages, verified local profile readiness, and visible authority signals that remain truthful. This change prepares Elyara Tours Granada to compete for high-intent Granada tour searches without inventing reviews, ratings, credentials, addresses, or guaranteed rankings.

## What Changes

- Add production-safe Search Console verification behavior and explicit sitemap submission readiness.
- Add localized legal and trust pages for visitor-facing credibility, including privacy/cookies/legal notice content appropriate for a static tourism site.
- Prepare publication of an approved Google Business Profile URL when configured, making it visible before exposing it in structured data.
- Strengthen authority and contact surfaces with approved public facts, while preserving the existing constraint against fake reviews, ratings, awards, business hours, physical addresses, or unsupported official-guide claims.
- Reconcile localized home SEO copy with the approved positioning so the home page can balance search intent and brand clarity.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `legal-trust-pages`: Localized legal and trust pages that improve visitor confidence and support compliant public contact/data handling information.

### Modified Capabilities
- `local-seo-presence`: Adds production ownership, approved GBP publication, and truthful authority-signal requirements.
- `static-tourism-site`: Updates localized public navigation, sitemap, metadata, and home SEO copy expectations for the new trust surfaces.

## Impact

- Affected content and routes: localized Spanish and English public pages, footer/navigation links, contact/authority pages, and home metadata.
- Affected SEO systems: `SeoHead`, sitemap generation, robots/search ownership metadata, JSON-LD business `sameAs`, and localized page alternates.
- No booking/payment system changes; reservations remain WhatsApp/email based.
- No dependency changes are expected.
