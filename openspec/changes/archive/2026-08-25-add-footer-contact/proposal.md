## Why

Visitors currently reach the end of the site without a persistent contact point beyond reservation CTAs. Adding footer contact details makes the business easier to contact from any page and gives visitors a direct social channel.

## What Changes

- Add a localized contact area to the global footer.
- Display the email address `elyaratours@gmail.com` as a `mailto:` link.
- Display Instagram as `@elyaratours` linking to `https://instagram.com/elyaratours`.
- Keep the footer lightweight and preserve existing footer navigation and language links.
- Do not add additional social networks, forms, backend contact handling, or newsletter signup.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `static-tourism-site`: Extend the global public footer with direct email and Instagram contact information.

## Impact

- Affected UI: shared footer in the base layout.
- Affected localized copy: contact heading label in Spanish and English if needed.
- No new dependencies, backend, form handling, or external APIs beyond simple links.
