## Why

The Malaga and Cordoba day-trip pages already imply private, tailored service, but the group positioning is not explicit enough on each origin-specific excursion. This change clarifies that these one-day excursions are for private groups so visitors understand the service format before requesting a proposal.

## What Changes

- Update the localized Malaga and Cordoba day-trip titles to explicitly position each excursion as a group/private-group offer.
- Reinforce the same group wording in SEO descriptions and introductory copy for the Spanish and English tour entries.
- Keep the existing inquiry-based pricing, external reservation flow, URLs, imagery, and day-trip landing structure unchanged.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `granada-day-trips`: Origin-specific Malaga and Cordoba day-trip detail pages must explicitly communicate that the excursions are designed for private groups.

## Impact

- Affected content: localized Markdown entries for Malaga and Cordoba day trips in Spanish and English.
- Affected display surfaces: tour cards, tour detail pages, SEO metadata, sitemap/structured-data descriptions where derived from tour content.
- No API, dependency, routing, booking, or payment changes.
