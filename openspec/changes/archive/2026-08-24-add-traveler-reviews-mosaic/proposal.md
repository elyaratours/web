## Why

The public site needs a warmer trust signal after visitors have reviewed the available tours. A small traveler reviews mosaic can make the experience feel more human and emotionally grounded without turning the page into a generic testimonial section.

## What Changes

- Add a localized reviews section after the tour catalog on the Spanish and English home pages.
- Present a small visual mosaic that interleaves traveler review cards with group photos.
- Allow visitors to click either a photo or review card to open an enlarged popup with the full image or full review text.
- Keep the initial mosaic intentionally small because only a limited set of approved group photos is expected at launch.
- Localize section copy and review content for Spanish and English, using adapted translations rather than forcing literal copies.
- Do not add social proof counters, statistics, internal booking, or payment behavior.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `static-tourism-site`: Adds the public traveler reviews and group-photo mosaic behavior to the localized tourism home experience.

## Impact

- Affects localized home page composition after the tours section.
- Affects localized copy for Spanish and English section titles and review content.
- May require a small data structure or content source for review/photo mosaic items.
- May require client-side behavior for an accessible popup/lightbox while preserving static-site generation.
- Requires approved group photo assets before launch content is final.
