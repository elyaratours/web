## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bottom-funnel Alhambra commercial SEO targeting
The system SHALL strengthen public commercial SEO targeting for bottom-funnel Alhambra searches while keeping all guide, price, ticket, and trust claims truthful and visible to visitors.

#### Scenario: Visitor opens Alhambra price or cost content
- **WHEN** a visitor opens localized public content targeting private Alhambra tour price, private Alhambra guide cost, or similar buying-intent searches
- **THEN** the content explains the visible price model or quote-request expectations in practical visitor language
- **AND** it states ticket inclusion or exclusion only when that fact is visible and approved on the page
- **AND** it provides direct WhatsApp or email inquiry paths rather than internal checkout or payment behavior

#### Scenario: Visitor opens official-guide Alhambra content
- **WHEN** a visitor opens localized public content targeting official Alhambra guide or official Granada guide searches
- **THEN** the content may identify Laura Hdez. León as a guía oficial de turismo or equivalent localized wording when that claim is visible and consistent with the authority page
- **AND** the content links or points to fuller authority context when appropriate
- **AND** it does not add a license number, jurisdiction, association membership, award, review rating, or third-party endorsement unless separately approved and visible

#### Scenario: Visitor opens ticket-availability planning content
- **WHEN** a visitor opens localized public content about sold-out, unavailable, or hard-to-find Alhambra tickets
- **THEN** the content gives realistic planning guidance and directs visitors to relevant route, contact, or tailor-made inquiry paths when available
- **AND** it does not claim Elyara can guarantee unavailable tickets, resell tickets, bypass official access rules, or provide internal ticket purchase checkout

#### Scenario: Search engine reads bottom-funnel Alhambra content
- **WHEN** a crawler reads localized bottom-funnel Alhambra content or structured data
- **THEN** visible content, metadata, and structured data use only approved public facts, visible prices or price caveats, direct contact channels, service area, language support, and approved guide facts
- **AND** the page does not emit hidden-only ratings, review counts, rankings, awards, addresses, business hours, ticket guarantees, or unsupported credentials
