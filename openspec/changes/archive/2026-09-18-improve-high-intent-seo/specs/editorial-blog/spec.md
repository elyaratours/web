## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: High-intent Alhambra editorial buying guidance
The system SHALL publish localized editorial guidance for Alhambra buying-intent questions that helps visitors understand cost, guide choice, ticket constraints, and inquiry options without duplicating tour detail pages.

#### Scenario: Spanish visitor researches Alhambra private guide price
- **WHEN** a Spanish visitor opens editorial content about the price or cost of a private Alhambra guide or private Alhambra tour
- **THEN** the article provides substantial Spanish visitor-facing guidance about what can affect the final quote, what is included or excluded, and how to request details through external inquiry channels
- **AND** it links visibly to relevant Spanish Alhambra tour, SEO landing, contact, tailor-made, or authority pages when those pages exist

#### Scenario: English visitor researches private Alhambra guide cost
- **WHEN** an English visitor opens editorial content about private Alhambra guide cost or private Alhambra tour pricing
- **THEN** the article provides substantial English visitor-facing guidance about what can affect the final quote, what is included or excluded, and how to request details through external inquiry channels
- **AND** it links visibly to relevant English Alhambra tour, SEO landing, contact, tailor-made, or authority pages when those pages exist

#### Scenario: Visitor researches official guide context for the Alhambra
- **WHEN** a visitor opens localized editorial content about choosing an official guide for the Alhambra or Granada
- **THEN** the content may reference approved guide facts that are visible elsewhere on the site
- **AND** it links to fuller authority context when appropriate
- **AND** it does not invent a license number, jurisdiction-specific authorization, award, review rating, or third-party endorsement that is not approved and visible

#### Scenario: Visitor researches sold-out Alhambra tickets
- **WHEN** a visitor opens localized editorial content about sold-out or unavailable Alhambra tickets
- **THEN** the article explains ticket-planning constraints, realistic next steps, and possible alternative Granada routes or inquiry paths
- **AND** it does not promise access to unavailable tickets, internal ticket sales, ticket resale, internal checkout, or guaranteed monument entry

### Requirement: Alhambra editorial cluster linking
The system SHALL connect Alhambra editorial buying guidance to matching localized commercial discovery pages, tour detail pages, related articles, authority content, and external inquiry actions.

#### Scenario: Article supports an Alhambra commercial landing
- **WHEN** a localized Alhambra article explains a topic served by a localized Alhambra SEO landing page
- **THEN** the article includes visible contextual links to that landing page and relevant tour detail pages
- **AND** the matching landing page may display the article as supporting visitor context

#### Scenario: Blog listing presents new Alhambra buying guidance
- **WHEN** the localized blog listing is generated after high-intent Alhambra editorial content is published
- **THEN** the listing presents the published articles with localized titles, excerpts, imagery when available, category labels, publication dates, and links to detail pages
- **AND** unpublished drafts remain absent from public listings, sitemap entries, and generated pages

#### Scenario: Search engine reads Alhambra editorial metadata
- **WHEN** a crawler reads a published high-intent Alhambra article
- **THEN** the page exposes localized article metadata, canonical URL, language alternates when a translation exists, breadcrumb structured data, article structured data, and social preview metadata based on visible page content
- **AND** metadata does not claim ratings, review counts, third-party endorsements, unsupported credentials, ticket availability, or guaranteed ranking outcomes unless those facts are approved and visible
