## Why

Elyara needs a dedicated public section for private one-day trips to Granada from nearby cities, starting with Malaga/Costa del Sol and Cordoba. This opens a new commercial entry point while preserving the current positioning: private, cultural, personalized experiences with an official guide rather than mass bus excursions.

## What Changes

- Add a new localized section/category for one-day private trips to Granada: `Day Trips to Granada` / `Excursiones de un dia a Granada`.
- Add a localized category landing page that explains the concept and lets visitors choose a departure city and Granada experience.
- Add localized public detail pages for private day trips from Malaga/Costa del Sol to Granada and from Cordoba to Granada.
- Present two main Granada experience options on day-trip pages: private Alhambra and Generalife, or Historic Centre plus Albaicin as a complete alternative.
- Present optional add-ons that help the visitor build their day: private transfer, selected local restaurant lunch, and tailor-made itinerary.
- Show pricing as `Price on request` / `Precio bajo consulta`, with no fixed price until group size, pickup city, transfer, tickets, lunch, and selected experience are known.
- Preserve external inquiry and reservation flows through WhatsApp and email, with CTAs such as `Request your private day trip` / `Solicita tu excursion privada`.
- Keep the current Elyara visual system, responsive layout, localized navigation, clean URLs, and basic SEO metadata.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `granada-day-trips`: Public localized section and pages for private day trips to Granada from other cities.

### Modified Capabilities

- None.

## Impact

- Affected app areas: localized navigation, localized route helpers/copy, tour/content collections, tour listing/category presentation, tour detail rendering, sitemap generation, and SEO JSON-LD for the new landing/detail pages.
- No new runtime dependencies are expected.
- No internal booking, payment, fixed-price, restaurant-menu, or monument-ticket availability system is introduced.
