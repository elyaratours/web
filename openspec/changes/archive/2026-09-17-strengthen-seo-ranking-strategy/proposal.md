## Why

The site already has a solid technical SEO foundation, but stronger search performance requires clearer commercial targeting, deeper intent-specific content, better internal linking, and consistent local trust signals. This change focuses on improving relevance and authority readiness for searches that can lead to reservations, without promising first-place rankings.

## What Changes

- Reposition the localized Home metadata and visible introductory copy toward high-value guided-tour searches such as Granada guided tours, private tours, Alhambra, Albaicin, and cultural walking routes.
- Expand priority SEO landing pages with deeper visitor-facing sections, stronger comparison/planning guidance, FAQs, and links to related tours, articles, and contact actions.
- Add or expand editorial cluster content around high-intent Granada planning topics that naturally connect to commercial landing pages and tours.
- Strengthen visible local trust signals using approved Google Business Profile, real approved reviews, direct contact channels, service area, and language support.
- Preserve ethical SEO constraints: no guaranteed ranking claims, fake reviews, invented ratings, unapproved credentials, physical address, or unsupported booking/payment promises.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `local-seo-presence`: Strengthen commercial local SEO targeting, Home intent metadata, landing-page depth, internal links, and approved local trust signals.
- `editorial-blog`: Expand editorial SEO topic clusters with visitor-useful articles that support commercial discovery and link to relevant tours/landings.

## Impact

- Affected content: localized Home copy, SEO landing Markdown, selected blog Markdown, related-content metadata, and local trust/review surfaces.
- Affected SEO output: page titles, meta descriptions, visible headings, internal links, FAQ JSON-LD where visible FAQs are added, sitemap-discovered pages, and structured data derived from approved visible content.
- No new dependencies, no internal booking/payment flow, no route churn unless a new published editorial article is intentionally added with a localized public slug.
