## Why

The site now exposes contact details in the footer, but visitors do not have a dedicated page that explains how to contact Elyara for questions, private routes, or trip planning. A lightweight contact page improves trust and discoverability while keeping contact channels limited to email and Instagram.

## What Changes

- Add localized contact pages at `/es/contacto/` and `/en/contact/`.
- Present a simple contact experience inspired by pateandogranada.net/contacto, adapted to Elyara's existing visual language.
- Display only the approved contact channels: `elyaratours@gmail.com` and Instagram `@elyaratours`.
- Link email with `mailto:elyaratours@gmail.com` and Instagram with `https://instagram.com/elyaratours`.
- Add localized navigation/discovery links to the contact page from the header and footer.
- Add localized SEO metadata and language alternates for the contact pages.
- Do not add WhatsApp, phone, physical address, forms, extra social networks, newsletter signup, backend handling, accounts, checkout, or payments.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `static-tourism-site`: Extend the public static website with localized contact pages and contact-page navigation.

## Impact

- Affected pages: new localized contact pages.
- Affected shared UI: header/footer navigation and localized contact labels/copy.
- No new dependencies, backend, form handling, storage, payment, or external API integration.
