Granada was a frontier, a capital and a symbol. On this tour, we follow the transition between two worlds: the Andalusi city reaching the end of its political era, and the Christian city reshaping its spaces, institutions and stories.

The route connects squares, streets and monuments in the historic centre to understand how continuity and rupture existed side by side after 1492, and how that tension still forms part of Granada’s identity.

We will explore the historic centre of Christian Granada before entering the Albaicín, where the legacy of the Muslim city can still be felt.
