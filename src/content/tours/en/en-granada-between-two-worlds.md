Granada was frontier, capital and symbol. On this route we follow the passage between two worlds: the Andalusi city reaching its political end and the Christian city reshaping its spaces, institutions and narratives.

The walk connects squares, streets and monuments in the historic center to understand continuity and rupture after 1492, and why that tension still forms part of Granada's identity.
