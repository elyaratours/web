Discover the many faces of Granada on a route through the neighbourhoods that shaped its identity over the centuries. Each area tells a different part of the story: the Christian city centre, the Albaicin rooted in the Islamic city, Sacromonte with its caves and popular memories, and the Darro as a visual thread between the Alhambra and the lower town.

This is a broad route for understanding Granada beyond its monuments. Along the way we connect history, culture, landscape and local life: how the Muslim medina was transformed, why squares and churches changed the city after 1492, how viewpoints explain both geography and memory, and why Granada changes when it is walked slowly.

The route includes the Historic Centre, Albaicin, Sacromonte and one of Granada's most iconic walks along the Darro. It is not only a sequence of viewpoints: the aim is to understand why each neighbourhood looks toward the Alhambra differently and how those relationships shaped the image of Granada.

The Albaicin has slopes and some uneven paving, but the pace includes pauses, explanations and time to look around. It is a good choice for a first visit, for travelers who want to orient themselves, and for private groups looking for history, atmosphere, photographs and cultural context in one route.
