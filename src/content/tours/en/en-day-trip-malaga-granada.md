Travel from Malaga or the Costa del Sol to Granada for a private cultural day shaped around your group. This is not a mass coach excursion: the proposal is built around your timing, interests, preferred pace and the Granada experience you want to enjoy with a cultural guide.

The day can begin with optional pickup at your hotel or an agreed point, followed by private transfer to Granada. Once in the city, choose one main experience: a private Alhambra and Generalife visit, or a private walking route through the Historic Centre and Albaicin.

The Alhambra option depends on monument ticket availability. If tickets are available, the visit focuses on the palaces, gardens, symbolism and history that shaped Nasrid Granada. After the visit, the itinerary may include free time, lunch or a calm complementary activity if the schedule allows.

The Historic Centre and Albaicin option is a complete alternative to the Alhambra. It explores Granada through its cathedral surroundings, historic streets, viewpoints, neighborhoods and layers of memory, without forcing too much into one day.

You can also add a local lunch experience at a carefully selected restaurant. The choice depends on availability, preferences and budget, so no fixed restaurant or menu is advertised in advance.

At the end of the day, return to Malaga or the Costa del Sol by private transfer if included in your proposal.
