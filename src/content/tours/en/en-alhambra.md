**The Alhambra is more than a palace — it is a world shaped by beauty, power and symbolism. As we move through its courtyards, halls, gardens and viewpoints, we uncover the stories hidden in its architecture and the ideas that defined Nasrid Granada.**

**This tour goes beyond dates and decoration. We talk about the rulers who built the Alhambra, the meaning of water and light, daily life inside the palace city, and the way the monument still speaks to the Albaicín, the landscape and the history of Granada.**
