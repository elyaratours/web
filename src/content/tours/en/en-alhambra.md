A slow, story-led route through the Alhambra as palace city, fortress and living symbol of Granada. We move through courtyards, gardens and viewpoints while reading the landscape around us.

This tour is designed for travelers who want context, not just dates. We talk about dynasties, water, architecture, daily life and the visual relationship between the Alhambra, the Albaicin and Sierra Nevada.
