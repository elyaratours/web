Granada for Families is a short, calm route through the Historic Center designed so children can discover the city without rushing and without turning the visit into a heavy lesson.

The walk explains, with simple language and visual stops, how the old Muslim medina gradually became a Christian city. We will look at streets, squares, symbols and buildings to understand how history can also be told as an adventure made of layers.

The visit is adapted to family pace: it lasts between one hour and one hour and fifteen minutes, leaves room for questions and focuses on ideas children can picture easily. It is a gentle way to introduce younger visitors to Granada's history while adults also find context and details that help them see the city differently.

The price is 7 EUR per person and children under 6 join for free.
