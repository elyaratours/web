The Cathedral and Royal Chapel tell the story of Granada's new political and religious project after the Christian conquest. This route focuses on their spaces, symbols and protagonists to read the city through power and memory.

We talk about the Catholic Monarchs, the transformation of the city center, Renaissance architecture and the role of these monuments as the stage for a Granada that wanted to present itself differently to the world.
