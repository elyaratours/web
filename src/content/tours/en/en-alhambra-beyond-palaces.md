The Alhambra is more than its most famous rooms. This visit approaches the complex as a palace city, a fortress, a water system and a constructed landscape designed to express power and beauty.

We pause at symbols, inscriptions, gardens, viewpoints and threshold spaces to understand how people lived and ruled from the red hill, and why the Alhambra still speaks to Granada centuries later.
