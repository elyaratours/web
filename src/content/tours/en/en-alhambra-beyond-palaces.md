The Alhambra is much more than its most famous rooms. This tour invites you to see the complex as a palatine city, a fortress, a water system and a carefully designed landscape created to express power and beauty.

We will focus on symbols, inscriptions, gardens, views and transitional spaces to understand how people lived and governed from the Red Hill, and why the Alhambra continues to shape its relationship with Granada centuries later.

We will explore the exterior areas of the Alhambra to discover the legacy of Charles V, the essential role of water, the Nasrid palatine city, its towers and some of the legends that have shaped its history.
