Asking how much a private Alhambra guide costs is a sensible step before booking. The short answer is that a useful quote depends on more than one number: group size, language, date, real duration, available tickets and the kind of guidance you want all matter.

Elyara's [Alhambra: Symbols, Power and Beauty](/en/tours/alhambra/) visit is presented from €150 per group, with tickets not included. That gives an orientation, but the date, number of people, preferred language and ticket situation still need to be reviewed before anything is confirmed.

## What can affect the cost

A private visit is different from joining an open group. The guide reserves time for one specific group and can adapt pace, explanations and questions. The final quote may vary if the group is larger, if another language is needed, if the request is very specific or if the visit has to be coordinated around tickets you already have.

Content also matters. A first-time general explanation is not the same as a visit focused on Nasrid art, water, symbolism, gardens or the relationship between the Alhambra and Granada. If you need a more customized proposal, the [tailor-made trips](/en/tailor-made-trips/) page is the right place to describe your priorities.

## Tickets are separate from the guide

The website does not sell tickets or take internal payments. When the visit includes Alhambra spaces with capacity limits or time slots, tickets are a separate practical point that must be checked carefully. If you already have tickets, mention your Nasrid Palaces time if it appears on your booking; if not, the visit has to be shaped around real availability.

This is why a WhatsApp or email inquiry is not automatic confirmation. It is the first step to review whether the date, group, language and tickets make a serious visit possible, without promising access that Elyara cannot control.

## How to ask for a realistic quote

Before writing, prepare four details: approximate date, group size, language and whether you already have tickets. If the Alhambra timing does not work or tickets are not available, another cultural reading of Granada through the Albaicin, viewpoints or historic center may be a better plan.

The goal is not an automatic checkout. It is to understand whether a realistic visit is possible for your group and what would be included before moving forward.
