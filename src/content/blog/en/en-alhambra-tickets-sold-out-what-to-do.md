Seeing that Alhambra tickets are sold out does not mean you should accept any promise. The Alhambra works with visitor limits, named tickets and specific time slots. If your date is unavailable, the most important thing is to keep the plan realistic and clear.

Elyara does not sell tickets through the website, does not offer resale and cannot guarantee access to sold-out areas. An inquiry is useful to review the situation, understand what tickets you have or can obtain, and decide whether an Alhambra visit or another cultural route in Granada makes more sense.

## First check what ticket is missing

Not every ticket situation is the same. Access to the Nasrid Palaces may be missing, the available time may not fit your plans, or a partial ticket type may still exist. Before writing, gather your date, group size, language and any confirmation or screenshot of the availability you have seen.

If you already have a ticket, also mention the Nasrid Palaces time if it appears on your booking. That detail shapes the whole day and helps avoid an impossible route.

## Avoid guaranteed-access promises

When a date is sold out, be careful with anyone promising access without explaining conditions. An official tourism guide can help you understand the monument and shape a visit, but an inquiry should not become a guarantee of tickets that are not available.

## Alternatives if you cannot go inside

Granada can still help you understand the Alhambra even when interior access is not realistic. An [Albaicin tour](/en/tours/albaicin/) shows the neighborhood facing the palace city; viewpoints explain its place in the landscape; and the historic center gives context for later political and cultural changes.

You can also consider a [tailor-made trip](/en/tailor-made-trips/) if the whole day needs to be reorganized. Sometimes a clear alternative is better than spending the visit trying to force access that the guide cannot control.

## How to write about your options

When you contact Elyara, include your date, group size, language, whether you already have any ticket and how important the Alhambra is in your trip. With those details, a realistic option can be suggested: a visit if it is viable, a timing adjustment if it fits, or an alternative route if access is unavailable.

The key is not to confuse guidance with confirmation. Until availability and conditions are reviewed, no serious proposal should promise tickets, internal payment or guaranteed entry.
